## 🔐 SAFETY GUARANTEE

### Diagnosis Prevention
```
User: "I have frequent thirst and weight loss"
Model would say (WRONG): "You likely have diabetes"
Model actually says (RIGHT): "These symptoms MAY indicate diabetes, 
but only a blood test can confirm. Please consult a doctor. ⚠️"
```

### Prescription Prevention
```
User: "How do I treat diabetes?"
Model would say (WRONG): "Take Metformin 500mg twice daily"
Model actually says (RIGHT): "Treatment options include medications. 
Your doctor will determine the right choice for you. ⚠️"
```
